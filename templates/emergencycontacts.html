Frequently Asked Questions

If you're arrested, remember these important steps:

  • Stay calm and do not resist arrest, even if you believe it's unjustified
  • Exercise your right to remain silent - you are not required to answer questions
  • Ask for a lawyer immediately and do not speak to police without your lawyer present
  • Do not sign any documents without legal counsel
  • Call a family member or friend as soon as you're allowed to make a phone call

If you've experienced or witnessed police misconduct:

  • Document everything - take photos of any injuries, write down badge numbers, officer names, time, location, and witness information
  • Seek medical attention immediately if injured and request detailed medical reports
  • File a formal complaint with the police department's internal affairs division
  • Contact the civil rights division of your local district attorney's office
  • Reach out to civil rights organizations that specialize in police misconduct cases

Legal emergencies typically include situations where immediate action is required to protect rights, safety, or prevent irreparable harm:

  • Imminent eviction or foreclosure
  • Domestic violence or abuse situations requiring protection orders
  • Arrests or detention by law enforcement
  • Workplace discrimination or harassment requiring immediate intervention
  • Child custody emergencies or child welfare concerns
  • Immigration detention or deportation proceedings

There are several options for obtaining free or low-cost legal assistance:

  • Legal Aid Society - provides free legal representation to low-income individuals
  • Public Defender's Office - for criminal cases if you cannot afford an attorney
  • Pro Bono Legal Clinics - many law firms and attorneys offer free services
  • Law School Legal Clinics - staffed by law students supervised by licensed attorneys
  • Bar Association Referral Services - can connect you with attorneys offering reduced fees
  • Legal Helplines - many organizations provide free legal advice via telephone
